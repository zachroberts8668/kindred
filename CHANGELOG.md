# Kindred Forever

## v1.1.0

First release.

- A character friends list for WoW Forever, separate from Battle.net. Add friends by First + Last name; the list is shared across all your characters.
- Online/offline status for every friend, with "last seen" time and zone.
- Friends who also run Kindred share their level, class, zone and AFK/DND status, and show as class-colored dots on your world map.
- Title Friends show their status, level and zone, even inside dungeons.
- Online, offline and level-up alerts, with per-friend muting.
- Groups, notes, search, and sorting by status, name, level or zone.
- Right-click a friend to Whisper, Invite, Who, or send a Title Friend request.
- "Add to Kindred" in player right-click menus (chat, party, raid, target, /who).
- Add-back prompt when someone adds you: Add, Not Now or Never.
- Privacy: your zone and map position are only shared with people on your list. Map sharing can be turned off in Options.
- `/ff debug` opens a copyable box for bug reports.
- Commands: `/kin`, `/ff`, `/kindredforever`.
